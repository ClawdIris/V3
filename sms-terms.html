SMS Program Terms

Casabe LLC · Effective August 24, 2026

Casabe LLC ("we", "us") sends SMS text messages through the Casabe Konnect shipment-management platform to customers who opt in to shipment notifications. This page describes the program.

How you opt in

You provide your phone number while creating a shipment order with a participating Casabe Konnect shipping office. You opt in either by checking the SMS consent box on the order or invoice form (the box is unchecked by default), or by giving consent in person at the counter, which office staff record on your order. Every consent is stored with a timestamp. SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase.

What we send

Messages are transactional and customer-service only: shipment and order confirmations, tracking and delivery updates, appointment and pickup coordination, and replies to your questions. We do not send marketing or promotional messages. Messages may be in English or Spanish.

Frequency and rates

Message frequency varies with your shipment activity, typically 2–6 messages per shipment. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How to opt out or get help

  • Reply STOP at any time to cancel. You will receive no further messages.
  • Reply START to re-subscribe after opting out.
  • Reply HELP for assistance at any time.
