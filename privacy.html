Privacy Policy

Casabe LLC · Effective August 24, 2026

Casabe LLC operates the Casabe Konnect shipment-management platform used by package-forwarding and courier offices. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

We collect the information needed to process shipments: names, phone numbers, delivery addresses, and shipment details. Where you opt in to SMS updates, we also store your consent record, including the consent method and a timestamp.

How we use phone numbers

We use phone numbers solely to send the transactional shipment notifications and customer-service replies you consented to: shipment and order confirmations, tracking and delivery updates, appointment and pickup coordination, and support conversations. We do not use your number for marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Mobile opt-in data and SMS consent records are not shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Service providers that deliver messages on our behalf (such as our SMS carrier partner) process this data under contract and only to provide the service.

SMS and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent records, including phone numbers collected for SMS, will not be shared with any third parties except service providers that process messages on our behalf solely to deliver the SMS program.

SMS message frequency varies with shipment activity, typically 2–6 messages per shipment. Message and data rates may apply.

Your choices

  • Opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP to any message; reply START to re-subscribe.
  • Request access to or deletion of your information by emailing casabexpressny@gmail.com.

Retention

We retain shipment records and consent records for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and accounting obligations, then delete or anonymize them.
